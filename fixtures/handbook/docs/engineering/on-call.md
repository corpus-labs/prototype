---
title: On-call and incidents
description: Who is on call, and what to do when a site breaks.
---

One person is on call each week. The rotation lives in the team calendar.

## When something breaks

- Post in `#web-incidents` before you start fixing.
- Write down what you see and when you saw it.
- Hand off out loud if your shift ends during an incident.

## Afterward

Write a short review within two days: what happened, how it was found, and what changes. Add anything that changes how the team works to the [decision log](../team/decisions.md).
