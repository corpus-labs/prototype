title: Content style guide
description: How campus sites sound, from headings to dates.
---

Write for someone scanning on a phone between classes.

## Headings

Use sentence case. Make each heading say what the section answers.

## Links

Link text says where it goes. Avoid “Click here” and “Learn more”.

## Dates and times

Write “March 4, 2027” and “2 p.m.” Leave out the day of the week unless it matters.
