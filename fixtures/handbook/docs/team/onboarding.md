Welcome. Your first week is for reading and pairing, not shipping.

## First week

- Read the [content style guide](../content/content-style.md) and the [accessibility review](../quality/accessibility-review.md).
- Pair on a [deploy](../engineering/deploying.md) with whoever is [on call](../engineering/on-call.md).
- Pick a small fix from the backlog and ship it with a reviewer.

## Accounts

Ask your lead for access to the repository, the deploy dashboard, and the team calendar.
