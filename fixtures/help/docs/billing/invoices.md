---
title: Invoices and receipts
description: Download an invoice, or add your company details to it.
---

Every order sends a receipt to the email you bought with.

## Download an invoice

Open **Account**, then **Orders**, and choose **Download invoice** next to the order. Invoices are PDFs.

## Add your company details

Add your company name, address, and VAT number under **Account**, then **Billing details**. New invoices include them. For an older invoice, reply to its receipt email and ask for a new copy.
