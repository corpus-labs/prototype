---
title: Licenses and sites
description: What a license covers, and how to move it to another site.
---

A license covers one live site, plus as many local and staging copies as you need.

## Move a license to a new site

Deactivate the license on the old site under **Settings**, then **License**. Activate it on the new site with the same key. You can move a license as often as you like.

## Use one theme on several sites

Buy one license per live site, or an unlimited license for client work.
