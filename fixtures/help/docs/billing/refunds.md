---
title: Refunds
description: How to ask for a refund, and how long it takes.
---

If a theme or plugin doesn’t work for you, ask for a refund within 30 days of purchase. Reply to your receipt email with your order number.

## How long refunds take

Refunds take 3 business days once we approve them. Your bank may need a few more days to show the money on your statement. Before March, refunds took 5 to 7 days.

## What we can’t refund

Orders older than 30 days, and renewals you’ve already used to download an update.
